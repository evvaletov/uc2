`v3.0.0-alpha.12`. The implementation is described in the
[release record](../releases/3.0.0a12-qa.md); the earlier project-wide repairs

## Review closure

Fresh GPT-6 Astra, Grok 4.7 Build Fast and Kimi K3 calls reviewed complete frozen
source bundles. Each seat worked independently and inspected source; reviewers
did not independently execute the reported tests.

- The production CLI metadata change had no confirmed blocking or substantive
  findings in rounds 1 and 2, with identical source hashes.
- Harness findings led to stronger lock/disk-fill guards, corrected SQLite
  checkpoint targeting, complete restore inventories, exact history checks after
  retries, packed manifest boundary checks and explicit shared-chunk corruption
  assertions. The final harnesses had no confirmed blocking or substantive
  findings in consecutive rounds 5 and 6.
- The elegance review covered state ownership, temporary index lifetime,
  equality/bounds checks and consequential complexity. Lists retain node ownership;
  temporary indexes accelerate lookup, and version counts are finalised once.
  Recovery cases now share one final history-and-verification postcondition.
- Two optional harness polish notes remain: saving partial corruption receipts
  sooner and constructing the v2 manifest hook inside its branch. Neither changes
  successful-run validity. Incomplete reviewer calls were retained separately and
  did not count towards clean rounds.
- Task-at reviewed the release record, snapshot recovery guide and public Sphinx
  roadmap. Its second round had no confirmed prose issues after adjudicating
  optional wording preferences. Later promotion TODOs and this evidence receipt
  are outside that frozen prose scope.

The [code/elegance ledger](alpha12-2026-11-14/reviews.json) records source and

## Validation

Local validation passed 36 CTests, 239 Python tests with two Windows-only skips,
the CLI metadata regression under ASan/UBSan, the assertion-side-effect lint and
the Sphinx warning check. All four workflows passed on the release commit:
[native builds/tests](https://github.com/evvaletov/uc2/actions/runs/43596167002),
[distributions](https://github.com/evvaletov/uc2/actions/runs/43596167104),
[integrations](https://github.com/evvaletov/uc2/actions/runs/43596167034) and
[browser/docs](https://github.com/evvaletov/uc2/actions/runs/43596167161).

The [scale and recovery evidence](../benchmarks/2026-11-14-scale-recovery.md)
includes two complete 3.25 GiB logical snapshot workflows, three independent
restore inventories per run, actual default entry/reference/manifest boundaries,
nine SIGKILL checkpoints and five actual ENOSPC/SQLITE_FULL cases. The second
large run and recovery reruns use the final reviewed harnesses. The snapshot
implementation itself is unchanged from the audited baseline.

The large corpus contains approximately 1 GiB of unique data. Fixed limits,
exclusive writable repository locking and manual stale-lock recovery remain.
Process-kill results do not establish hardware power-loss durability; the 1 TiB
byte ceiling and the chunk/history inventory caps were not boundary-tested.
